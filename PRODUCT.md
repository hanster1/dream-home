# Product

**Who:** anyone who has daydreamed about "the house." It's for delight and inspiration, not a CAD tool.

**Job:** in under a minute, go from "where would I live?" to a believable, beautiful picture of the house. Then keep it, share it, and compare it with other ideas.

**Flow:** Setting (5 categories × 3 sites) → Architecture (6) → Plan (single story, two story, cantilever, courtyard) → Scale (2,400 to 12,000 sq ft) → Reveal (exterior, interior, twilight, plus a summary card with beds, baths and an illustrative build estimate).

**Principles**
- Every image is photoreal and reviewed. Nothing renders at random.
- The landscape is the hero. Architecture previews are shown in your chosen category.
- Be honest about substitutions. Approximate images are labeled as such.
- No accounts. Saved homes live in the browser, and a share link carries the whole design.

**Not yet**
- Plan and scale don't change the photo yet. They drive the copy, the stats and the estimate.
- No live AI generation: that would need a server-side key and a proxy.
- Twilight covers 6 of the 30 exteriors.
